# PyQuest Provisional Terms of Service Outline

> **PROVISIONAL DRAFT OUTLINE — SUBJECT TO QUALIFIED LEGAL COUNSEL REVIEW**  
> This outline defines the operational terms, user obligations, and educational disclaimers for PyQuest. It does not constitute formal legal terms. Formal drafting and legal review by qualified counsel are required prior to public launch.

---

## 1. Educational Purpose & Grading Disclaimers

1. **Practice-Based Learning Platform:**
   - PyQuest is an educational tool provided for self-paced Python practice.
   - Code execution occurs directly inside the learner's web browser using the Pyodide WebAssembly runtime.
2. **Untrusted Practice Checks (Tier A):**
   - In-browser exercise evaluations and automated test runs are provided solely for instant instructional feedback.
   - Tests and check suites are bundled with the curriculum and are readable within the client environment.
3. **Self-Reported Progress & XP (Tier B):**
   - XP, levels, streaks, and badges record personal effort and progress milestones.
   - **Crucial Disclaimer:** Completion claims are self-reported by the client application. Server checks (including attempt timers and idempotency validations) ensure structured data storage, but **they do not verify code authenticity, correctness, or independent student mastery**.
   - PyQuest XP and achievement badges **do not constitute accredited academic credentials, official certifications, or verified exam results**.
   - Public competitive rankings and verified credentials are not offered in the MVP.

---

## 2. Eligibility & Account Rules

1. **Age Eligibility (Pending Decision D1):**
   - *Option A (Local-Only for Minors):* Learners under 18 years of age may use the platform in Guest / Local-Only mode without creating an account or storing personal data on our servers. Server accounts are restricted to individuals aged 18 and older.
   - *Option B (Consented Accounts):* Learners under 18 may create accounts only with verifiable parental or guardian consent.
   - *Option C (Staged Guest-First):* All users participate locally; account creation requires satisfying age gating and parental approval criteria.
2. **Account Responsibility:**
   - Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials.
   - Accounts must use pseudonymous display names generated by the system; impersonation or offensive handles are prohibited.
3. **Single Account per Learner:**
   - Learners agree not to register multiple accounts to manipulate platform metrics or claim duplicate rewards.

## 3. Acceptable Use Policy

Learners agree not to:
1. Attempt to bypass, tamper with, or flood platform API endpoints, including the completion claim route (`/api/exercises/:type/:id/claim`).
2. Script automated attacks, denial-of-service attempts, or vulnerability scans against PyQuest infrastructure.
3. Attempt to exploit the browser execution environment to disrupt other users or escape the Web Worker sandbox.
4. Distribute or inject malicious payloads, abusive content, or unauthorized automated bots into platform forms.
5. Exceed published rate limits (e.g., maximum permitted claims per minute or signup attempts per hour).

## 4. Service Availability & Limitations

1. **"As-Is" Service:**
   - PyQuest is provided free of charge on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis without warranties of any kind.
2. **Infrastructure Constraints:**
   - Certain platform features depend on free-tier infrastructure. Unused projects or cold runtime loads may experience latency or temporary unavailability.
   - We reserve the right to limit access, enforce rate limits, or adjust reward rules to preserve service stability.
3. **Account Termination:**
   - Users may terminate their account at any time via the self-service account deletion tool (`DELETE /api/account`).
   - We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate the Acceptable Use Policy.
